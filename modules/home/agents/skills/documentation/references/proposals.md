# Proposals

Help a reader decide whether to approve proposed work. Establish the problem, intended decision, audience, constraints, and evidence of the current state before drafting a solution.

## Flexible Skeleton

1. **Problem and Desired Outcome.** Explain the concrete issue and a checkable success condition.
2. **Current State and Constraints.** Separate verified facts from assumptions needing confirmation.
3. **Recommendation.** Describe proposed behavior, scope, and rationale. A recommendation is not an approved decision.
4. **Alternatives and Tradeoffs.** Include credible options relevant to the decision, with evidence for comparative claims.
5. **Execution and Validation.** Describe steps, dependencies, rollout, and recovery when relevant. Label estimates; owners and dates need confirmation.
6. **Risks and Open Questions.** Identify uncertainties that affect feasibility or approval.
7. **Decision Needed.** State what the reader needs to decide.

Use `clarifying.md` for missing requirements or unresolved choices. Offer a labelled recommendation when helpful and let the user make the decision. Do not present a drafted proposal as a recorded, approved, or implemented decision. Update its status only from explicit evidence.
